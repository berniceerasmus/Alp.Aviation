Superlatives

✈ SOUTH AFRICA · TURKEY · BEYOND — Ranking the Best Summer Holidays
01

Quick Recap Warm-Up

5 min

Fast recap of comparatives from last lesson, then pivot the whole group toward ranking. Ask around the room:

"Whose summer holiday was farther from home?" · "Who had a hotter holiday — you or your neighbour?"

Then ask the pivot question that sets up today's grammar: "OK — but out of everyone in this room, who went the farthest?" Let students realise a comparative (two people) doesn't work here — we need a new structure for three or more.

Teacher note — This pivot question is the whole point of the warm-up: it creates a real need for superlatives before you name them. Don't rush past it even if no one answers confidently.
02

Grammar Focus: Superlatives

12 min

What is a superlative?

Use a superlative when comparing three or more people, places, or things — to say one is "number one" in the group (the biggest, the best, the most expensive).

TR Üç veya daha fazla şeyi karşılaştırırken kullanılır — "en" anlamı taşır.
Short adjectives (1 syllable)
the + adjective + -est
Turkey was the hottest destination this August.
Long adjectives (2+ syllables)
the most + adjective
Cape Town is the most beautiful city on the trip.

Spelling reminders

big → the biggest (double the consonant)  ·  sunny → the sunniest (y → i)  ·  safe → the safest (just add -st)

Irregular forms

AdjectiveSuperlative
goodthe best
badthe worst
farthe farthest / the furthest

"One of the..." — a natural upgrade

Native-sounding speakers often soften a superlative with one of the + superlative + plural noun, especially when they don't want to sound too absolute.

Bodrum is one of the most popular beach towns in Turkey.
Kruger is one of the best safari parks in the world.
TR "One of the..." yapısı — "...lerden biri" anlamına gelir, kesinlik biraz yumuşatılır.
Teacher note — Elicit first: "What's the superlative of hot?" before giving the rule. Flag that superlatives almost always need "the" — a very common omission error at this level. Keep this stage tight; the board activity is where real practice happens.
03

Guided Practice: The Departures Board

10 min

Superlatives need three or more items to compare — so today's board adds a third destination. Project or print the board below as shared "data" for the sentences that follow.

✈ TOP SUMMER DESTINATIONS BOARD
CategoryTurkeySouth AfricaSpain
Flight time from Istanbul—≈ 10–12 hrs≈ 4 hrs
Typical August weatherHot / dryCool / winter seasonHot / dry
General cost levelLowerHigherMid-range
Crowds in AugustVery crowdedQuieterCrowded
Popular activityBeaches & seaSafari & wildlifeCity breaks & beaches
Note: general seasonal patterns — South Africa's winter falls during the Turkish summer.

Students complete the sentences using the board and the correct superlative form. Click Show to reveal an answer during class.

  • Of the three, South Africa is ___ (far) destination from Istanbul. the farthest
  • Turkey is ___ (cheap) of the three destinations for a Turkish traveller. the cheapest
  • South Africa has ___ (good) reputation for wildlife safaris. the best
  • Turkey is ___ (crowded) destination in August out of all three. the most crowded
  • Spain is ___ (close) destination to Turkey by flight time. the closest
  • Many travellers say Cape Town is one of ___ (beautiful) cities in the world. the most beautiful
  • Out of the three, August tourism to South Africa is ___ (bad) fit — it's winter there. the worst
  • For a quick weekend trip, Spain is ___ (convenient) option for Alp Aviation staff. the most convenient
Teacher note — Do items 1–3 together as a class, then let students try 4–8 in pairs before revealing. Watch for missing "the" (a very common error: "South Africa is farthest" instead of "the farthest") and for "goodest/bestest" style over-regularisation.
04

Speaking: Which Holiday Wins?

10 min

In groups of 2–3, students rank the categories below for South Africa vs. Turkey as a summer holiday, then defend their ranking using comparative and superlative sentences.

Beaches
Food
Nightlife
Safety
Value for money
Nature & wildlife
Sentence starters: "I think ___ is more ___ than ___ because..."  ·  "In my opinion, ___ has the most/best ___..."  ·  "For me, the biggest difference is..."

Bring the class back together: each group shares one comparative and one superlative sentence about their ranking.

Teacher note — Push stronger students for opinion language ("I'd argue that...", "Honestly, I think..."). For a quieter group, assign each pair one category instead of all six.
05

Wrap-Up: Exit Ticket

3 min

Go around the group — each student says one superlative sentence about their own summer, real or imaginary.

Example: "The best part of my holiday was the food in Bodrum."

Close by naming the two structures one more time: comparative (-er / more... than) for two things, superlative (the -est / the most) for three or more.

TR Kısa özet: iki şeyi karşılaştırırken "daha", en üstünü söylerken "en" kullanılır.
